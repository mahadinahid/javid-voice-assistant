Providers

Models, in the order they’re tried

Allowances are in each provider’s own unit, from config/providers.yaml; the ones not checked against your account say “unverified”. At 80% of an allowance a provider is kept in reserve (used only when the others fail), at 95% it’s off until its reset, and a provider that answered with a rate limit rests until its retry-after time. Your calendar, tasks, memory and past chats only go to providers marked personal_ok: true there. Every call is counted in data/providers.db. Token counts are Groq’s own numbers for requests made through Javid; other apps sharing a key aren’t included. Your model order is saved in llm_chain.json.
